Seven reasons your home services website isn't ringing the phone right now

Generic web design agencies don't get trade businesses. Here's what they miss.

1

After-hours calls go to voicemail hell

A pipe bursts at 9pm. They Google "emergency plumber." Your site has the phone number buried 3 scrolls down. They call the next guy who picked up first.

2

You're invisible on "[trade] near me"

76% of local searches end in a call within 24 hours. If you're not in the Google Maps 3-pack, you're not getting the call. Period.

3

Tire-kickers from 50 miles away

No service area map = phone calls from places you don't even cover. Wasted time, frustrated customers, lost real leads while you're explaining geography.

4

Your Google Business Profile is a ghost town

No photos. No services listed. 12 reviews from 2019. Your competitor across town has 200. Guess who Google ranks first.

5

Customers can't tell you do quality work

No before/after photos. No project gallery. No reviews on the homepage. Customers can't see proof, so they pick the guy who shows them proof.

6

Your site looks like 2014

Outdated design tells customers you might be outdated too. A modern site does the silent work of building trust before anyone reads a word.

7

Your quote form asks for a novel

14 fields, full address, square footage, three preferred call-back times. Customers bail at field 5. Smart forms ask for the 4 things that matter and capture the lead in 30 seconds.
